PayPal

The quickest option — pay with your PayPal balance, bank account or any card PayPal accepts. No account is strictly required at checkout.

  1. Add your items to the cart and open the cart page.
  2. Press the gold PayPal Checkout button — a secure PayPal window opens.
  3. Log in (or choose “Pay with card”) and confirm the total.
  4. You’ll return here with a confirmation, and we’ll email your order details.

Payments go to {{ $pp.business }}.

MetaMask Ethereum

Send ETH, USDC or USDT on the Ethereum network via MetaMask.

  1. Scan the QR code with your MetaMask app, or copy the address below.
  2. Send the agreed amount in ETH, USDC or USDT.
  3. Reply to our email with the transaction hash.
  4. We confirm on-chain and ship your order.
0x4E9D527492E7f3D8b3356b69828e6FDe01EE9084

Ethereum network only — funds sent on another chain can be lost.

Solana Wallet SOL

Send SOL or SPL tokens on Solana — fast and low fees.

  1. Scan the QR code with your Solana wallet (Phantom, Solflare, Backpack…).
  2. Send the agreed amount in SOL or USDC.
  3. Reply to our email with the transaction signature.
  4. We confirm on-chain and ship your order.
