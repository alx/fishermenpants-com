## Where we ship from

Every order is packed by hand at our shop in **Lamai Beach, Koh Samui, Thailand** and posted through the Thai postal service (or EMS when you choose it).

## Delivery times

| Destination | Standard | EMS express |
|---|---|---|
| Thailand | 2–4 business days | 1–2 business days |
| Asia | 10–18 business days | 5–7 business days |
| Europe & UK | 14–24 business days | 7–10 business days |
| USA, Canada & Australia | 14–28 business days | 7–12 business days |

**Free standard shipping on orders over $149 USD.** Below that, standard shipping is a flat **$18 USD** per parcel — that is roughly what a single parcel costs us to send from Koh Samui, and it is shown as a separate line in your cart before you pay.

These are typical times — customs checks and local postal delays can add a few days, especially in December and around Thai public holidays.

**EMS tip:** express shipping is expensive for a single item. It is much better value with two or more pieces. Ask us for an EMS quote before you order and we will tell you the exact cost.

## Tracking

Standard shipping does not include a tracking number. **EMS always includes tracking**, and we email it to you once the parcel is on its way.

## Customs and import charges

Orders are sent from Thailand as personal purchases. Depending on your country, customs may charge import duty or VAT before delivery. These charges are set by your government, not by us, and are the buyer's responsibility. We declare parcels accurately — we cannot mark an order as a gift or under-value it.

## Returns

We want you to be happy with your purchase.

- **Changed your mind?** Contact us within **14 days** of delivery. Items must be unworn, unwashed, and in their original condition.
- **Wrong or damaged item?** Email us with a photo and we will replace it or refund you in full, including shipping.
- **Return postage** is paid by the buyer unless the item was faulty or we sent the wrong thing.

We cannot accept returns on items that have been worn, washed, or altered.

To start a return, email us at [thaifishpants@gmail.com](mailto:thaifishpants@gmail.com) with your order reference (it looks like `FP-261116-ABCD`) and a short note about the issue.

## Refunds

Once we receive and inspect a return, we refund to your original payment method within **5 business days**. PayPal refunds usually appear in 3–5 days; card refunds can take up to 10 days depending on your bank. Crypto payments are refunded in the same currency at the rate we received.
