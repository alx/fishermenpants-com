## A shop by the sea

GAYA sits on Had Lamai Road, a few steps from the water on the east coast of Koh Samui. The shop has been open since **2003** — it started as a small stall and grew into the shop it is today, still run by the same hands and still filled with things we would wear ourselves.

We sell what the island gives us: light cotton and hemp, hand-dyed indigo, embroidery from hill-tribe makers, and the fisherman pants that started it all.

## Made to travel light

The fisherman pants are the reason most people find us. They are wrap pants in the original Thai sense — no zip, no buttons, a generous waist that ties at the front and draws at the ankle. You can wear them long to the beach, short in the heat, or pulled up as a jumpsuit. They fold to almost nothing, which is why they end up in every traveller's bag.

Everything else in the shop follows the same idea: pieces that survive being rolled into a rucksack, washed in a bucket, and worn for a month straight.

## Made by hand, in small runs

Our stock is not mass-produced. The tie-dye is done by hand, batch by batch, so no two pieces are identical — the pattern you see on the product page is the one you receive, and it may differ slightly from the next one made. Sizes are one-size and cut generously, which makes them forgiving to buy without trying on.

## Come and see us

If you are on the island, come by. We are open every day from **10:00 am to 7:00 pm**, and we are always happy to talk about the pieces, the fabric, or where to eat nearby.
