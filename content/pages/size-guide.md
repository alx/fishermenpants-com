Most of what we sell is **one size**, cut generously — the fisherman pants in particular are made to wrap and tie, so a single size fits a wide range of bodies. The measurements below come from the garments themselves, measured flat.

## Kaftans & dresses

Cut loose and flowing, one size. They are intended to drape rather than fit closely — if you are between sizes or tall, expect a shorter, more fitted look.

## How to measure

Use a soft tape measure, and measure over light clothing:

- **Chest** — around the fullest part of the bust, keeping the tape level.
- **Waist** — around your natural waistline, the narrowest point above your hips.
- **Length** — from the shoulder seam straight down to the hem.
